Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity. A simple website might take 4-6 weeks, while more complex applications can take 2-3 months or longer. We'll provide an exact timeline during the discovery phase.

What is your payment process?

We typically require a 50% deposit to start the project, with the remaining 50% due upon completion. We also offer flexible payment plans for larger projects.

Do you provide hosting services?

While we don't directly provide hosting, we recommend reliable hosting providers and can assist with the setup and configuration of your website.

Can you help with website maintenance?

Absolutely! We offer comprehensive maintenance and support packages to keep your website secure, updated, and performing optimally.

What technologies do you work with?

We're proficient in a wide range of technologies including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Python, and many more. We choose the best tech stack for your specific needs.
